# 7-Minute Demo Script

## Minute 1

Ask: "How are you currently tracking drive eligibility and student follow-up?"

## Minute 2-3

Show the TPO dashboard:

- Total students
- Upcoming drives
- Students eligible
- Mock tests taken this week
- Students needing attention

## Minute 4-5

Open a drive and show:

- Criteria
- Eligible count
- Results or shortlist status

## Minute 6

Open the student list and point out:

- Profile completeness
- Mock attempt count
- Readiness gaps

## Minute 7

Ask: "What would make this immediately useful for your placement cell?"
