# PlacementIQ One-Pager

## Placement Season Chaos?

Most TPO teams are dealing with:

- 500+ students, multiple companies, and too much Excel
- Repeated "Am I eligible?" questions from students
- Incomplete profiles and last-minute follow-up
- No quick visibility into who is actually prepared

## What PlacementIQ gives you

- Instant drive eligibility visibility
- A readiness dashboard for TPO follow-up
- Mock-test activity and profile completion in one place
- Basic student reminders without manual chasing
